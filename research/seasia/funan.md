# Funan

- **Region:** Southeast Asia
- **Span:** 50 CE to 627 CE
- **Source:** Wikipedia: Funan 50-627 CE (Chenla conquest 627)

## Key events

- **313 CE:** Edict of Milan — Christianity legalized
- **453 CE:** Attila dies — Hunnic Empire fragments
- **476 CE:** Western Rome falls
- **610 CE:** Muhammad receives first revelation
- **627 CE:** Chenla conquers Funan — Southeast Asia's first state absorbed
- **636 CE:** Battle of Yarmouk — Rashidun defeat Byzantines; Syria lost to Rome
- **650 CE:** Khazar Khaganate forms — the steppe's Jewish kingdom
- **651 CE:** Sasanian Empire falls — Yazdegerd III killed at Merv
- **668 CE:** Silla + Tang destroy Goguryeo — Korea's Three Kingdoms era ends
- **682 CE:** Srivijaya's earliest inscription (Kedukan Bukit)
- **751 CE:** Battle of Talas — Abbasids defeat Tang; papermaking spreads west
