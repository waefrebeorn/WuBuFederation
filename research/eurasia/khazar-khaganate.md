# Khazar Khaganate

- **Region:** Eurasia
- **Span:** 650 CE to 969 CE
- **Source:** Wikipedia: Khazar Khaganate c.650-969 (conquered by Kievan Rus 965-969)

## Key events

- **650 CE:** Khazar Khaganate forms — the steppe's Jewish kingdom
- **651 CE:** Sasanian Empire falls — Yazdegerd III killed at Merv
- **668 CE:** Silla + Tang destroy Goguryeo — Korea's Three Kingdoms era ends
- **682 CE:** Srivijaya's earliest inscription (Kedukan Bukit)
- **751 CE:** Battle of Talas — Abbasids defeat Tang; papermaking spreads west
- **793 CE:** Lindisfarne raid — the Viking Age begins
- **800 CE:** Charlemagne crowned Holy Roman Emperor
- **882 CE:** Oleg the Wise makes Kiev the Rus capital
- **935 CE:** Silla surrenders to Goryeo — Korea unified
- **960 CE:** Kingdom of Aksum collapses
- **969 CE:** Kievan Rus destroys the Khazar Khaganate
- **1050 CE:** Cahokia's Big Bang — population surges 10x
- **1054 CE:** Great Schism — East and West churches split
- **1066 CE:** Battle of Hastings — the Viking Age ends
- **1096 CE:** First Crusade
