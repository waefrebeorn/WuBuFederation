# Etruscans

- **Region:** Italy
- **Span:** 900 BCE to 27 BCE
- **Source:** Wikipedia: Etruscan civilization 900-27 BC; Villanovan culture

## Key events

- **776 BCE:** First Olympic Games
- **700 BCE:** Monte Alban founded — Zapotec civilization rises
- **551 BCE:** Confucius born (551 BCE); the Buddha's lifetime (c. 563–483 BCE) overlaps
- **490 BCE:** Battle of Marathon — Greece repels Persia
- **336 BCE:** Alexander the Great begins conquest
- **221 BCE:** Qin unifies China; Great Wall begun
- **209 BCE:** Modu Chanyu founds the Xiongnu Empire
- **100 BCE:** Teotihuacan founded — the Americas' greatest city rises
- **27 BCE:** Augustus — Roman Empire begins
- **18 BCE:** Baekje founded — one of Korea's Three Kingdoms
- **30 CE:** Crucifixion of Jesus; Christianity spreads
