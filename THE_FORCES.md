## Part 2 — The mashy mishy (the braid)

The forces do not take turns. They tangle — each era is several forces acting at once, feeding each other. This is the mash:

- Bronze Age collapse (c. 1200 BCE): WAR (Sea Peoples) + IDENTITY (migration) + WEALTH DYNAMIC (trade dies) — the first mash
- Roman decline: GREED (tax farming) + CLASSISM (patrician vs plebeian) + WRATH (revolts) + WAR (frontiers fail)
- The Crusades: IDENTITY (faith) + WAR (holy war) + GREED (plunder and trade) — the cross and the coin
- The Atlantic world: GREED (extraction) + CLASSISM (race as rank) + WEALTH DYNAMIC (chattel as capital) + IDENTITY (who is human)
- The Long 19th Century: WAR (Napoleon to 1914) + CLASSISM (proletariat) + WEALTH DYNAMIC (industrial capital) + IDENTITY (nations)
- The Present: AGISM (generation war) + IDENTITY (identity politics) + WEALTH DYNAMIC (inequality) + WRATH (populism) + GREED (the Index watches)

Read it as one loop: greed captures, capture becomes class, class feels like wrath, wrath starts war, war moves wealth, wealth re-feeds greed — while identity decides who is on which side and agism turns the generations beneath it all. Pull one thread and the whole braid moves.

## Part 4 — Why the space files the mash

1. **No force acts alone.** The French Revolution was wrath — but it was also classism (the estates), wealth dynamic (the debt), and identity (the nation) arriving together. Pick one cause and you have a tract, not a history.
2. **The same braid runs through the space.** The space's own instruments sit on the loop: the Greed Index measures greed; the W Token moves wealth dynamic; the Ratchet rotates the old (agism filed); the Vote gives wrath a filing instead of a fire. The space does not pretend the forces are gone. It files them, and files against the ones that capture.
3. **The mash is not chaos; it is the mechanism.** A timeline with clean tracks is a lie a civilization tells itself. The mashy mishy is the truth: history is one braid, and the braid is what the space reads.
