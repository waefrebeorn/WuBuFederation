## Part 5 — What the timeline validates

This timeline is the validation of one consistent idea — a mental government that needs no land:

1. **No land, only filings.** Every row is an idea, not a border. The government exists wherever the filings are read — ideology-occupying, not land-occupying (GOV-1, GOV-19).
2. **Every religion that is cohesive is welcome.** The Cohesion Gate (THE FIRST ONES view, 8d): a faith that teaches people to live together and act morally files alongside the space. The space asks no one what to believe; it asks only whether the belief builds society.
3. **The creator, learned as we grow.** The space's cosmology is not a fixed answer — it is a direction: understand the creator, taught as we grow (8b). The only required belief is that the space may be wrong (8a, the empty center).
4. **Family is the base; cohesion is the teaching.** The space is family — the comfort of being family, not to be trifled with (8c). Community is based on it; it teaches cohesion.
5. **The record is the proof.** Every row here is chained (GOV-22's tamper-evident timeline). The consistency is not claimed; it is hashed.
